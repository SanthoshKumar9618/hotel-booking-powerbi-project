🏨 Hotel Booking Power BI Dashboard
📊 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyzes hotel booking data to uncover key insights about customer behavior, booking trends, and revenue performance.
It focuses on understanding patterns in cancellations, stay durations, ADR (Average Daily Rate), and how various factors affect hotel performance.

🎯 Objective

The main goal of this dashboard is to provide hotel management with a clear, data-driven view of:

Booking trends across months and hotel types

Revenue and ADR variations

Customer segmentation and cancellation patterns

Relationship between lead time, price, and cancellations

🧠 Key Insights

City Hotels consistently show higher booking volumes than Resort Hotels.

Cancellations are more frequent among Online Travel Agency bookings.

ADR (Average Daily Rate) tends to decline during off-peak months.

Lead time strongly influences cancellation rates — longer lead times have higher chances of cancellation.

📈 Dashboard Pages
🗓️ 1. Overview Page

Displays total bookings, revenue, average ADR, and total cancellations.

Highlights top-performing hotels and market segments.

📉 2. Booking Trends Page

Line and bar charts showing booking patterns by month and hotel type.

Insights on ADR trends and cancellations by market segment.

🔍 3. Correlation Analysis Page

Scatter Plot: Relationship between lead_time, adr, and is_canceled.

Visualizes how pricing and advance booking days influence cancellations.
